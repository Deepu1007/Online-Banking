About Our Digital Banking System

Our Digital Banking System is designed to provide a seamless, secure, and modern banking experience. Along with regular banking features, we introduce a powerful and unique addition — the Family / Group Account Feature, allowing families or grouped individuals to manage shared financial activity effortlessly.

🔹 Concept of Family / Group Account

A Primary Account Holder (Parent/Head/Admin) can create a shared digital wallet that links multiple sub-accounts such as children, spouse, or trusted group members. The admin controls spending, monitors activity, and manages fund allocation for all sub-accounts.

🔹 Roles in Family / Group Account

1. Admin / Parent (Primary Account Holder)

Creates the family/group wallet, adds members, allocates funds, sets spending limits, and views reports.

2. Member / Child (Sub-Account Holder)

Can spend only within limits, request extra funds, and track simple balance history.

🔹 Key Features of the Family Account System

Create Family Wallet

Admin forms a Family ID and links multiple users under one digital wallet.

Add Members

Sub-accounts are added with their own virtual wallet IDs.

Fund Allocation

Allowances can be one-time or recurring, like ₹2000 for Child1 or ₹5000 for Spouse.

Spending Limits

Daily or monthly limits ensure safe financial control.

Expense Tracking

Real-time reports and notifications for every transaction.

Request Extra Funds

Members can request more money; admin can approve/reject instantly.

Parental Controls

Block categories or set time-based spending restrictions.
